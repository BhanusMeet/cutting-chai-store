Our Story

One kettle, one corner, one big idea

It began in the winter of 1994 with a single steel kettle, a kerosene stove and a stool borrowed from the neighbour. The idea was plain: a proper cup of chai, boiled with patience, handed over with a smile, for the price of a small coin.

The city was already running late, with commuters rushing to the 8:42 and shopkeepers rolling up their shutters. Everyone needed a minute. We gave them a cutting.

Half a glass of chai is a full conversation.

Why “cutting”? Because in Bombay a full glass was a luxury and half was a habit, shared between two friends and one newspaper. We kept the glass small and the stories big.

Today the same recipe simmers in the same slow way: strong leaf, fresh ginger, crushed elaichi, buffalo milk, and not a minute less than it deserves. The cassette player still sits on the shelf. The songs are the old ones.
