By Our Head Roaster · A Notice to the Discerning Drinker

There is a particular hour, just after the second crack, when a good roast turns from green to gold to the deep brown of old furniture, and the whole shop smells of toasted sugar and woodsmoke. It is that hour we wish to put in your cup.

Every bag of Ember & Oak is roasted in lots of no more than twelve kilos, over a fire tended by hand. We buy only from growers we have met, and we pay them fairly. Nothing sits upon a shelf; your parcel leaves our door within a day of the roast.

“Roasted this morning, on your doorstep within the week.”

Choose a parcel below and we shall do the rest: a fresh bag, or several, arrives each month, ground to your liking or left whole. Pause, change or cancel at any time by sending us a line; no questions, and no ceremony.
