Our Story · By the Proprietors

In the spring of 1912, Josiah Holloway hung a green and gold sign above a narrow shopfront on Mercer Street and opened his dispensary to a town that needed one. He kept a ledger, a set of brass scales and a firm belief that a remedy ought to be made by someone who would look you in the eye the next morning.

His son Edmund came to the counter in 1938, after the lean years, and with him came the shelves of amber glass that still line the back wall. Through two wars and a good many hard winters, the shop never closed its door to a neighbour in need, whether or not the neighbour could pay that week.

“A remedy ought to be made by someone who will look you in the eye.”

Today the third generation, Margaret and Thomas Holloway, work the same oak counter and the same brass scales. The recipes are written in the original ledger, and we follow them to the grain. We have added a few things over the years, but we have taken nothing away.

Everything we sell is compounded in small batches on the premises, bottled by hand, and labelled in our own writing. We use honest ingredients, we say plainly what is in every jar, and we answer for every one we sell.
