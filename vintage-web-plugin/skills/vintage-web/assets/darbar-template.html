The Darbar of Suvarnagiri

Open to honoured guests, by grace of the House

A Royal Proclamation

Be it known to all who approach these gates that the fort of Suvarnagiri, long silent, throws open its halls once more. The Diwan-i-Aam shall receive guests at the hour of the morning drum, and the Sheesh Mahal shall be lit by a thousand lamps at dusk.

Every visitor shall be welcomed with attar and cardamom tea, and shown the Toshakhana where the treasures of four generations are kept. Let none arrive without a gift of good humour.

— By order of the Darbar, in the year of the House 1612

Sheesh Mahal

The palace of mirrors, where a single flame is multiplied into a night sky of ten thousand stars.

Zenana Mahal

The quarters of the royal household, with latticed jali screens that let the breeze in and keep the heat out.

Toshakhana
