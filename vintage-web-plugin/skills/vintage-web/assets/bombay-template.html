City Desk

Local train timetable gets a new face

The Western line unveiled its revised timetable on Thursday, with three additional fast locals between Churchgate and Borivali. Commuters at Dadar were seen comparing the new printed cards on the platform and arguing happily about the 6:12.

“It is the little changes that keep the city moving,” said a stationmaster, adjusting his cap against the afternoon sun.

Marine Drive lights go up early

The Queen’s Necklace was switched on a full half hour before sunset this week as the city prepares for the festive season. Chai sellers report brisk business along the promenade.
