Master Craftsmen Reopen the Old Mill to Great Acclaim

After forty years of silence, the looms turn once more, and the whole town turns out to see them.

By Our Special Correspondent · Illustrated

The doors of the old mill on Weaver’s Lane opened at nine o’clock sharp on Saturday morning, and by half past the street was filled with townsfolk eager to witness what many had believed they would never see again.

“It is a matter of patience and good thread,” remarked the foreman, adjusting his cap. “The rest is only practice.” The first bolt of cloth was presented to the mayor, who declared it the finest he had handled.

“A matter of patience and good thread.”

Orders have already been received from as far afield as the coast, and the proprietors have promised that every length will be inspected by hand before it leaves the premises. A modest exhibition of historic patterns is open to the public throughout the month.

Notices

Tours of the mill depart daily at ten and two. Apprentices are wanted; apply within.
